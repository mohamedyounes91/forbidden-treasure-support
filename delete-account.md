# Forbidden Treasure – Account & Data Deletion

**Last updated: September 24, 2026**

Forbidden Treasure allows players to permanently delete their account and associated game data.

## Delete Your Account From the Game

If you still have access to Forbidden Treasure, you can delete your account directly from the Game:

**Settings → Account → Delete Account & Data**

Review the confirmation message and confirm deletion.

After successful deletion, the account and associated Forbidden Treasure data are permanently removed.

## What Is Deleted?

Account deletion removes the Forbidden Treasure data associated with the account, including:

- Game account and profile
- Cloud-saved game progression
- Nickname reservation
- Global leaderboard entry
- Associated Firebase Authentication account
- Local progression belonging to the deleted account

Account deletion cannot be undone.

## Don't Have Access to the Game?

If you have uninstalled Forbidden Treasure, lost access to the device, or otherwise cannot use the in-game deletion option, you can request deletion of your account and associated data by email.

We may request reasonable additional information if necessary to verify that the request relates to the correct account before deleting data.

Once the account has been appropriately identified and the deletion request has been processed, the associated Forbidden Treasure account data will be permanently deleted.

## Data Retention

Forbidden Treasure account data is retained while the account remains active or as necessary to provide the Game's features.

After successful account deletion, the associated account data controlled by Forbidden Treasure is deleted.

Information processed independently by third-party service providers may remain subject to their own retention requirements and privacy policies.
