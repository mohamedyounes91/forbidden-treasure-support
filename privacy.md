# Privacy Policy for Forbidden Treasure

**Last updated: September 24, 2026**

Forbidden Treasure ("the Game") is developed and published by Mohamed Younes ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our").

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected or processed when you use Forbidden Treasure, how that information is used, and the choices available to you.

## 1. Information We Collect and Process

### Account Information

Forbidden Treasure supports Google and Guest accounts.

The Game uses Firebase Authentication to create and manage user identities. A unique Firebase User ID (UID) is associated with your account.

When Google Sign-In is used, authentication is handled through Google and Firebase Authentication.

Forbidden Treasure does not use your Google email address, real name, or Google profile photo as part of your Game profile.

### Nickname

Players choose an in-game nickname.

Your nickname is associated with your game account and may be displayed publicly on the global leaderboard.

### Gameplay and Progression Data

The Game may store gameplay information associated with your account, including:

- Best score
- Coins and Scarabs
- Power-up levels
- Character unlocks and selections
- Missions and mission progress
- Daily login progress
- Prize Wheel state
- Other persistent game progression and related preferences

Players using a Google account may have supported progression stored using Firebase cloud services so that their progress can be restored.

Guest accounts primarily rely on local device progression unless subsequently connected to a supported Google account.

### Leaderboard Information

Forbidden Treasure includes a global leaderboard.

Your selected nickname and best score may be publicly visible to other players.

A technical account identifier may also be used internally as part of the leaderboard record.

### Advertising and Device Information

Forbidden Treasure uses Google AdMob to provide optional rewarded advertisements.

Google advertising services may process information such as device or advertising identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID where available, and technical information such as device, network, and IP-related information.

Advertising-related data is processed according to Google's applicable privacy practices.

The Game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) where applicable to manage advertising privacy choices and consent.

Players can continue using the Game without choosing to watch rewarded advertisements.

### Security and Integrity

The Game may use Google/Firebase security services, including Firebase App Check and Google Play Integrity, to help protect backend services and reduce abuse.

These services may process technical device, app, network, and integrity-related information as necessary to provide their security functions.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information processed by Forbidden Treasure is used to:

- Create and manage game accounts
- Maintain player identity and nicknames
- Save and restore supported game progression
- Operate the global leaderboard
- Provide gameplay features
- Provide optional rewarded advertisements
- Manage advertising privacy choices
- Protect the Game and its backend services from abuse or unauthorized access
- Support account and data deletion

We do not sell players' personal information.

## 3. Third-Party Services

Forbidden Treasure uses services provided by Google, including:

- Firebase Authentication
- Cloud Firestore
- Firebase App Check
- Google Play Integrity
- Google AdMob
- Google User Messaging Platform
- Google Credential Manager / Google Sign-In services

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

Game account and supported cloud progression data may be stored using Google Firebase services.

We use reasonable technical measures intended to protect stored and transmitted information, including access controls and authenticated access to private account data.

Internet transmission and electronic storage cannot be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 5. Data Sharing

Certain data is transmitted to Google services used to provide authentication, cloud storage, security, and advertising functionality.

Public leaderboard information, including your chosen nickname and best score, can be visible to other players.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

## 6. Account and Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your Forbidden Treasure account from within the Game:

**Settings → Account → Delete Account & Data**

Deleting your account is intended to permanently remove the account and associated server-side game data controlled by Forbidden Treasure, including:

- Account profile
- Cloud game progression
- Nickname reservation
- Global leaderboard entry
- Associated Firebase Authentication account

Progression belonging to the deleted account stored locally by the Game is also erased as part of the deletion flow.

This action cannot be undone.

If you no longer have access to the Game, you may submit an account and data deletion request using the public deletion page provided on the Forbidden Treasure Google Play listing.

## 7. Data Retention

Account-related game data is retained while your account remains active or as necessary to provide the Game's features.

When account deletion is successfully completed, the associated Forbidden Treasure account data described above is deleted.

Information processed independently by third-party service providers may be subject to those providers' applicable retention requirements and policies.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Forbidden Treasure is not designed to knowingly collect additional personal information such as a player's real name, email address, postal address, or phone number for the purpose of creating an in-game profile.

If you believe that personal information relating to a child has been provided or processed inappropriately, please contact us using the address below so the matter can be reviewed.

## 9. International Processing

Services used by Forbidden Treasure may process information on servers located outside the player's country or region.

Where applicable, data processing by our service providers is subject to their respective safeguards and privacy terms.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game, its services, or applicable requirements change.

The latest version will be made available through the public Privacy Policy page, and the "Last updated" date will be revised when appropriate.
